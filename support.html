Support

Something broken, a word that will not behave, a question about setting a board up. It all goes to one address.

support@wordbridgeaac.com

What to include

Whatever you have is enough to start. These four make the answer faster:

  • What you were doing when it happened
  • What happened instead
  • The device, and which version of its system it runs
  • The app version, from Settings, then About
